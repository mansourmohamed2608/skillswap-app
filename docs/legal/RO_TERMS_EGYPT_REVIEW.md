# R&O Terms and Conditions: Egypt Review Draft

Status: proposed for business-owner and qualified Egyptian legal review. This document is not published application copy and does not change the existing Terms page or its July 26, 2024 date.

## Required confirmations before publication

- Legal entity name, registration details, registered address, and contact channel.
- Whether R&O acts only as a marketplace facilitator or is a party to any service exchange, product transfer, or fee payment.
- Payment provider, subscription terms, cancellation flow, refund process, and any applicable taxes or invoices.
- The official support email and escalation route for complaints, safety reports, privacy requests, and disputes.
- Data-controller role, hosting locations, processors, retention periods, and cross-border transfer details.
- The business-approved governing-law, dispute-resolution, and court-jurisdiction language for Egypt.

## Proposed R&O terms structure

1. **About R&O and acceptance.** Identify the verified legal operator and describe R&O as a platform for members to request and offer services, products, or fees. State that use requires acceptance of the terms and applicable laws.
2. **Accounts and eligibility.** Set account accuracy, age, verification, account security, suspension, and prohibited-use rules.
3. **Listings, exchanges, and user content.** Explain that members are responsible for listing accuracy, lawful conduct, permissions, and delivery of their own exchanges. Grant R&O only the limited license needed to host and operate user content.
4. **Safety and prohibited conduct.** Prohibit fraud, misleading listings, harassment, unlawful services, unsafe in-person exchanges, infringement, and sharing sensitive information. Include reporting and moderation paths.
5. **Payments, subscriptions, cancellation, and refunds.** Describe the actual provider, prices, renewal, cancellation, statutory consumer rights, and refund handling only after the business confirms the policy. Do not promise refunds or outcomes not operationally supported.
6. **Platform role and liability.** Clearly distinguish the platform from individual members. Preserve all non-excludable rights and avoid liability exclusions that are unenforceable under applicable law.
7. **Privacy and personal data.** Link to the reviewed privacy notice, explain the purposes of processing and user rights, and identify the support/privacy contact after confirmation.
8. **Complaints and disputes.** Give a verified complaint channel, reasonable handling process, and the approved governing-law/jurisdiction wording. Egyptian-law references require qualified legal review.
9. **Changes, termination, and contact.** Explain how members are notified of material changes and provide verified support and safety contact details.
